CSV to TSV Converter

Convert CSV data into tab-separated values (TSV) instantly in your browser. Your data stays on your device.

🔒 Privacy-friendly: Your data is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.

How to Use CSV to TSV Converter

  1. Paste your CSV data into the input box.
  2. Click Convert to TSV.
  3. Copy the TSV result or download it as a .tsv file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TSV?

TSV stands for Tab-Separated Values. It is a text format where columns are separated by tab characters instead of commas.

Does this tool upload my CSV data?

No. The conversion is performed locally in your browser, so your CSV data is not sent to a server by this tool.

Can I download the converted TSV file?

Yes. After converting the CSV, click the Download TSV button to save the result as a .tsv file.

Can CSV values contain commas?

Yes. Properly quoted CSV fields containing commas are supported.
