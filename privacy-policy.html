About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CSV & JSON Developer Tools handles information when you use our free browser-based developer tools.

Data Processing

Most tools on this website process your data directly in your web browser. Files and data entered into the tools are designed to remain on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to our server for processing.

Privacy First: Our developer tools are designed to process supported data locally in your browser whenever possible.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information simply to use our tools.

We may receive limited technical and usage information through third-party analytics services, such as information about pages viewed, approximate usage activity, device type, browser type, and similar technical information.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, including which pages are visited and how users interact with the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information.

Google processes this information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Microsoft Clarity

Some pages also use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the website. Clarity can process interaction events such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, along with page-rendering and DOM information for behavioral reports, heatmaps and session replay. It may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on the consent state and project settings.

The converter panels on the CSV to XML, CSV Viewer and Excel to JSON pages are explicitly marked for Clarity masking. Microsoft documents that masked content is not uploaded to Clarity. For details about Microsoft's processing and consent controls, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement, Clarity Consent Mode and Clarity masking documentation.
